Getting There

Location & Travel

Château de Sanse

Our venue for the weekend - an 18th century château set among vineyards and cypress trees in the Bordeaux countryside.

Address: 2624 Sanse, 33350 Sainte-Radegonde, France

View on Google Maps

Nearest Airport

Bordeaux-Merignac (BOD), approximately 1 hour 10 mins from the château by car.

Where to Stay

Nearby villages

These villages are all a short drive from the château - we recommend AirBnB for local stays in the countryside.

If you have any questions about where to stay, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

  • Gensac4 min drive
  • Combes, Juillac3 min drive
  • Pessac-sur-Dordogne5 min drive
  • Doulezon8 min drive
  • Flaujagues8 min drive
Transport between guest accommodation and the Château will be provided on the day of the wedding ceremony from nearby villages. Of course, if you wish to stay further afield that's totally okay too and there is plenty of parking at the château.
